Research overview

Explore uncertain futures through actuarial science, AI and the mathematics of time.

Actuarial Engineering — Parallel Futures is a research initiative designed to go beyond a single forecast and build rigorous models across many possible futures.

The initiative combines stochastic risk, actuarial decision-making, computational modelling and exploratory questions inspired by modern physics, while keeping the scientific boundaries explicit.

The initiative

A research programme, not just a single article.

This repository is intended to be the main home of the initiative. Individual papers, models, experiments and discussions will sit under one recognisable research identity.

Actuarial Engineering — Parallel Futures is built around a simple idea: actuarial science already studies uncertain futures, and modern computation now allows us to explore those futures more deeply, more transparently and more interactively.

The initial focus is practical and credible: AI-augmented stochastic risk models, counterfactual decision frameworks and computational actuarial applications. More exploratory questions about time and relativity can sit alongside these as clearly identified research streams.

The repository actuarial-engineering-parallel-futures will therefore function as the umbrella structure for the initiative, rather than creating unrelated standalone pages for each paper.

Physics studies what futures are possible. Actuarial science measures the consequences of uncertain futures. Actuarial Engineering builds systems that help us make decisions before those futures arrive.

Relativistic Life Insurance and Temporal Valuation

A theoretical study of mortality, valuation and contract design when biological time and financial time may not align in the same way.

View outline

Use proper-time mortality ideas and Earth-time discounting as a mathematically disciplined thought experiment, while separating proven relativity from speculative engineering application.

AI-Enabled Parallel Futures Simulation Engine

An auditable computational environment for scenario generation, valuation APIs and comparison of alternative risk decisions across many simulated futures.

View outline

Develop the architecture for scenarios, experiments, result logging, visual outputs and reproducible actuarial computation—using AI only where it adds measurable value.

What would have happened under different underwriting, pricing, reinsurance or reserving decisions—and how should actuaries evaluate those alternatives?

Investigate causal assumptions, look-ahead bias, intervention logic and practical methods for evaluating alternative historical or strategic choices within risk systems.

AI & computational lab

Ideas must become models.

The initiative is intended to produce working demonstrations, not only conceptual writing. Each important idea should eventually connect to a model, experiment or application.

Research approach

  • Start with a clear research question and explicit boundary conditions.
  • Build a conventional actuarial or statistical baseline before adding AI.
  • Test whether AI improves calibration, speed, insight or usability.
  • Keep methods auditable, reproducible and open to critique.
  • Translate the strongest ideas into APIs, applications or simulations.
