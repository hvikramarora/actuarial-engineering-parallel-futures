# Contributing — proposed policy (review before public launch)

This initiative welcomes collaboration from academics and industry practitioners. Discussion, issue participation or code contributions do **not** automatically confer authorship on a working paper.

## Contribution channels
- **GitHub Discussions:** concepts, literature, questions, critical review and researcher introductions.
- **GitHub Issues:** clearly scoped model and documentation tasks.
- **Pull requests:** proposed changes to models, data documentation or manuscripts.

## Research integrity
Claims should be supported by scientific evidence and primary references. Differentiate proven physical theory, speculative theoretical models, actuarial scenarios and empirical results. AI-generated material must be checked, appropriately disclosed where required, and never used to invent citations or data.

## Authorship
Discuss authorship criteria and expected contributions at the start of each paper. Use contribution records (such as CRediT) to support transparent discussion; agree author order with the contributors before submission. Participants not meeting authorship criteria can be acknowledged with their permission. The proposed policy must be reconciled with target journal requirements.

## Licensing and intellectual property
No general software or content reuse license has been chosen. Do not accept external code/manuscript contributions until appropriate licensing, ownership and consent terms have been finalized.
