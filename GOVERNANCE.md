# Governance — proposal for initial research initiative

**Initiative founder and initial coordinator:** Harsh Vikram Arora.

Each proposed paper will have its own author team, corresponding author, editorial workflow and publication decision. A contributor to one paper does not automatically join the author list of other papers.

Suggested early decisions:
1. Confirm the initiative's scientific scope and priorities.
2. Select paper leads and document expected contributions.
3. Agree authorship criteria, contribution records and dispute handling.
4. Adopt policies on data, privacy, AI use, manuscripts and code licensing.
5. Establish transparent review milestones and versioned releases.

This is a preliminary governance outline, not a representation that a research group or institutional partnership already exists.
